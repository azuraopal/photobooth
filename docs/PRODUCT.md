# Photobooth

## Product direction

A camera-first personal photobooth with expressive original frames, simple capture, and reversible editing. Warm paper, charcoal, and burnt orange. The supplied references establish layout selection, themed frames, and filter browsing; their artwork is not bundled.

## Implemented first slice

- 12 layouts covering 2/3/4/6 photos; six original typographic frame palettes.
- Seven color-matrix filters used consistently in camera preview and exported strips.
- Front/rear camera, countdown sequence, pose prompts, replace one shot, import from gallery.
- Caption editing, PNG export, private local collection, OS share sheet.
- Camera unavailable/denied handling with gallery import alternative.

## Differentiating roadmap

1. Pose story: themed sequence prompts with group-friendly timing; current version uses a fixed sequence, not AI analysis.
2. Motion memories: an optional short behind-the-scenes clip accompanying each strip. Not implemented yet; microphone permission should stay off by default.
3. Portal scenes: local subject segmentation with original themed artwork. Requires performance and edge-quality evaluation on physical Android and iPhone devices.
4. Event packs: custom event name, palette, date and private local gallery.
5. AR accessories: face tracking with opt-in effects; do not label ordinary stickers as AR.

## Acceptance

Keep every original photo, make retakes targeted, avoid cropping faces unexpectedly, show export failures without losing the session. A passing build does not prove camera quality, live AR, or gallery-sharing behavior on a physical device.
